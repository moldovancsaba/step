STEP turns the physical world into a map you earn by showing up.

The planet is divided into a fixed grid of triangles (the STEP MESH). Stand inside one, and STEP builds a cryptographically signed “proof of presence” — without ever sending your exact location off your device. Only the triangle’s id and a proof hash are submitted; independent validators check it and smart contracts finalise it. Be there first, and you mine the triangle, earning Trinity and a collectible slot NFT.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Mine the triangle you’re standing in — one tap, real presence required.
• Explore a live oasis/desert map: green triangles are open to mine, red ones are mined out.
• Strengthen a proof with a trusted anchor — tap a nearby Bluetooth beacon, NFC tag, or QR at participating locations.
• Keep your mined triangles in your wallet as NFTs, with full on-chain provenance.
• List, buy, gift, and cancel triangle NFTs in the marketplace (testnet).

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your precise location is processed on-device only and never leaves it.
• Your wallet is non-custodial: keys live in the iOS Keychain. Optional account backup is encrypted on your device with a password we never receive.
• No ads, no third-party trackers, no background location.

GENUINE-DEVICE PROOF
STEP uses Apple App Attest to confirm the app runs on real hardware, helping keep mining fair against spoofing and bots.

IMPORTANT
STEP is a testnet / pilot. Trinity is the protocol’s internal unit — it has NO monetary value, cannot be bought with money, and is not an investment. Blockchain records are public and permanent.

You must be 17+ and physically present to mine. Be honest, be there, and claim your corner of the map.
